Privacy Policy — Reels Scheduler

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Reels Scheduler is a personal tool operated by Matúš Kačmár (Prešov, Slovakia) that uploads short vertical videos to the operator's own Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube accounts at scheduled times.

What data the app accesses

  • Meta (Facebook / Instagram): the list of Facebook Pages you manage, the linked Instagram professional account, and permission to publish videos to them.
  • TikTok: basic profile info (open ID) and permission to upload videos to your account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to your channel (youtube.upload scope).

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and the schedule of posts are stored only in a local database on the operator's own computer or server. They are used solely to upload the videos you schedule. The app does not read, analyze or collect any other content, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share any data with third parties.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the app's access at any time at Google security settings.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access at any time in the settings of each platform (Facebook: Settings → Business Integrations; TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google: link above). To have all stored tokens and data deleted, contact mkacmar1@gmail.com; deletion is completed within 30 days.
